What is your current tech stack?

I specialize in deep full-stack engineering. My toolkit revolves around TypeScript, React, Next.js, and Node.js. For databases, I use PostgreSQL and Prisma. Most importantly, I breathe life into these platforms using advanced CSS and GSAP for sophisticated motion.

How do you handle complex state and architecture?

By engineering predictable data flows. I use Redux, Zustand, or robust React Context patterns alongside TRPC or GraphQL to ensure state mutations are safe, tracked, and scalable even during rapid iterations.

Can you build for high-traffic, production environments?

Absolutely. Developing for production means implementing safe deployment pipelines, enforcing rigorous typing with TypeScript, and optimizing logic. Performance is non-negotiable.

What is your approach to modern UI/UX design?

I view front-end as a tactile experience. Utilizing custom fluid systems and semantic markup, I ensure that the user’s cognitive load is minimal, guiding them seamlessly through content via smart motion.

Are you open to collaborative development roles?

Yes! The best digital experiences are built in synergistic teams. I love translating complex design specs into living code alongside talented creatives and engineers.
